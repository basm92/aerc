## Jingwen

1. The student describes/motivates the aim of the research, the main research question and connects this to societal challenges (societal relevance).

The student clearly states the research question and focuses on social relevance. However it is not clear from the introduction why infrastructure investments has improved people's lives and how - survey or anecdotal evidence could have been integrated to focus on this point. 

2. The student describes the contribution to the existing literature (scientific relevance).

The literature review features relevant and appropriate theory, however there is no good justification for East vs. West. This becomes important later on, when the difference in results between those two groups becomes apparent. 

3. The student motivates and explains the research approach (methodology)

The research methodology is explained, but it is very lengthy and some of the details could have been omitted. Sometimes choices are being made without arguments. Picking control variables by empirical selection is not always a good idea, since they do not take into account endogeneity concerns.

4. The student applies the research approach in an appropriate and correct way; if applicable,  insights and theories of the dedicated minor are incorporated in the research (approach).

The methodology is carried out in a correct way, however, the results are presented in a non-standard format, which makes interpretation confusing. Some tests are referred to as robustness tests whereas they don't serve such purpose. Heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors have to be applied by default. Some findings also suggest univariate regressions have been performed (separate R-squared statistics for each variables) rather than multivariate regressions. 

5. The student interprets research findings in the light of societal and scientific relevance (interpretation).

The findings are interpreted in terms of theory, but the discussion sometimes lacks depth. There is also little reflection about the policy recommendations and the counterfactual implications of the findings.
